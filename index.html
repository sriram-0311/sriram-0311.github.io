How robust is a robot foundation model?

I am measuring how NVIDIA's GR00T N1.7 vision-language-action policy degrades as visual, geometric, semantic and latency perturbations increase, on a humanoid pick-and-place task in Isaac Sim (IsaacLab-Arena). Paired seeds and Wilson confidence intervals at every perturbation level.

  • Camera blackouthead camera rendered black when the policy server shared the 16 GB GPU; fixed by moving inference to CPU and adding a per-run camera health check
  • Stale framethe first observation after an environment reset still showed the previous episode; a re-fetch fix moved success from 22% to 86%

Validating the evaluation pipeline came first: both bugs silently lowered the success rate without crashing anything. Perturbation sweeps are next.

Curb Rash Alert: from integration to production

I brought up Curb Rash Alert from early integration to production vehicles. The feature connects the perception algorithm on the ADAS ECU to the camera server on the infotainment ECU, which shows the alert. I validated the integration on the bench against CANoe-simulated system states.

Northeastern University · Toyota Research Institute collaboration

Multi-camera GPS-visual-inertial SLAM, and certifiable estimation in Isaac Sim

As a research assistant I developed the fast-tracking front end and global relocalization of a multi-camera GPS-visual-inertial SLAM system, in collaboration with Toyota Research Institute. KD-tree keyframe lookup cut relocalization latency, and Bag-of-Words loop closure bounds map drift.

Separately, I connected NVIDIA Isaac Sim to ROS 2 Jazzy through OmniGraph to stream multi-sensor data with exact ground truth, then replayed it into iSAM2 to evaluate GTSAM back ends that use QCQP/SDP relaxations to certify globally optimal trajectories.
